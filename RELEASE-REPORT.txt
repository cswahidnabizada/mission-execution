DECISION / HONEST SCOPE
The accepted 16.10.40–16.10.44 roadmap has been consolidated into one source candidate. It is NOT truthful to claim every roadmap capability is production-complete in this single release. The draft editor, typed composition schema, token/component editing, page/journey composition, market variants, responsive preview frame, validation, and JSON export are implemented. Canonical server-side draft persistence, immutable version lifecycle, step-up publication, verification, rollback, full existing-route composition integration, and browser visual/accessibility certification remain unimplemented or unverified. Publish is explicitly disabled. Do not treat this candidate as the finished live Layout Studio.
